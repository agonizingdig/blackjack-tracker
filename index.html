This page reads your own blackjack logs from Torn's official API. It runs entirely in your browser — there is no server behind it.

I don't have a key yet

Blackjack history lives in your Torn logs, and only two kinds of key can read them:

  • A Full Access key — reads your entire account. Do not use one here.
  • A custom log-only key — reads logs and nothing else. Much narrower, and the option this tool is built for.

Torn's settings page only offers four fixed levels, and none of them is "logs only". The only way to create a custom key is a special link.

Read this before clicking.

The link below immediately creates a live API key on your account. There is no confirmation step and you cannot edit it afterwards — only copy or delete it.

The key it creates can read all of your Torn logs, not just blackjack. That includes trades, money movements and market activity. It cannot spend your money, move your items, or act in the game — but it is a detailed history of what you have done.

It is still considerably narrower than a Full Access key, which is the only alternative that reaches logs at all. That is the trade-off. You do not have to use this button.

You can delete any key at any time from Settings → API Keys.

Things worth knowing

Your data

Cached history lives in this browser only. Clearing your browsing data erases it — a backup saves you re-downloading.

How this tool behaves

It only reads.
It calls Torn's official log endpoint to read your own history. It does not play hands, place bets, or take any action in the game. The endpoint it uses is read-only — it has no ability to act on your account.
It stays under the rate limit.
Torn permits 100 API calls per minute. This page throttles itself to 60 and shows its current rate in the top corner, so it stays well clear of the ceiling.
Your key does not leave your browser.
This is a static page with no backend. Your key is sent to api.torn.com and nowhere else. Nothing is logged, collected or transmitted to the person who made this.
Nothing downloads on its own.
History is only fetched when you press a button, and only for the range you ask for.
Judge it against the rules yourself.
The above describes exactly what this page does. It is not a claim about what Torn's rules permit — that is yours to check, and the rules can change.
